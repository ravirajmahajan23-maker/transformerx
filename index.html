04 / Transformer operation

Parallel operation & load sharing

Estimate how the total load is shared by two transformers using their complex impedances.

Transformer and load details

Enter percentage impedance as R% + jX%. Both transformers are assumed to have compatible voltage ratios, polarity, phase sequence and frequency.

I₁ / I₂ = Z₂ / Z₁   (after converting both impedances to a common base)
The calculation assumes equal secondary voltage and compatible transformer connections. If the transformers have unequal voltage ratios or phase displacement, circulating current must also be considered; this simplified calculator does not model it.
